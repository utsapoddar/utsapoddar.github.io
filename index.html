About Me

Physics taught me to distrust a result I can't account for. That habit shapes how I build: models report their uncertainty instead of a single confident number, the deterministic part of a system stays in charge of the decision, and anything I claim about performance is measured rather than assumed.

I hold a BS in Physics from the University of Calgary and am completing an MS in Data Science at CU Boulder, with the university's AI certificate stacked inside the degree. Most of what I build ends up open source — pipelines, Bayesian estimators, and agent tooling you can read and run yourself.

DopaKernel

Open Source

Agent Architecture · LLM Tooling

A semantic completion controller for AI agents. It freezes the objective into prioritized, verifiable requirements, allocates the next action from explicit progress and information state, and refuses to report completion until every requirement passes a fresh verifier — so a perceived better idea cannot quietly replace the instruction. Its core allocation rules come from four primary results on dopamine prediction-error coding: three adopted, one documented as rejected.
